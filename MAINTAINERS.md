# Overview

Maintainers are trusted members of the Stay Elite community who help ensure the quality, stability, and long-term success of our open-source projects.

Maintainers are expected to lead by example, encourage collaboration, and foster a welcoming community.

---

# Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing Pull Requests.
- Managing Issues and Discussions.
- Triaging bug reports.
- Reviewing feature requests.
- Maintaining documentation.
- Helping contributors.
- Ensuring code quality.
- Maintaining project security.
- Planning releases.
- Keeping dependencies up to date.

---

# Expectations

Maintainers should:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Follow the Code of Conduct.
- Review contributions fairly.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Encourage community participation.
- Make decisions transparently.
- Prioritize project stability over feature quantity.

---

# Becoming a Maintainer

Maintainers are selected by the Organization Owners.

Selection is based on:

- Consistent contributions
- High-quality code
- Helpful code reviews
- Positive community engagement
- Technical expertise
- Reliability

There is no minimum number of contributions required.

Quality is valued over quantity.

---

# Maintainer Inactivity

Maintainers who become inactive for an extended period may have their maintainer permissions removed to ensure projects remain actively managed.

Inactive maintainers are always welcome to return.

---

# Stepping Down

Maintainers may step down at any time by notifying the Organization Owners.

Former maintainers remain valued members of the Stay Elite community.
